Anime Card Farm Crafting: Honest Limits

Crafting UI, when present, is easy to get wrong on fan wikis because recipe lists drift. This page states what we will and will not publish.

Short answer: If your client shows a recipe, follow those labels. Ignore third-party craft lists that do not match your UI. We do not invent material costs or output cards.

What players should do

  1. Redeem codes that grant production / luck / mutation potions first.
  2. Compare production upgrades vs packs with cost ÷ income gained (packs).
  3. Open crafting only when the board already produces (cards).
  4. Ignore third-party “craft lists” that do not match your UI labels.
  5. If a recipe consumes a card you still need for offline, stop and re-read offline money.

Crafting vs mutations

Do not craft and mutate in the same unmeasured minute. Sequence experiments so mutations notes stay clean.

Corrections

Send UI-matched recipes to hello@animecardfarm.wiki. We would rather publish fewer recipes than a confident wrong grid. Policy: editorial, sources.

Why we keep this page short on recipes

Incomplete recipe grids that look complete are worse than an honest limits page. Players craft away key cards, then blame offline systems. Until we can maintain UI-matched recipes with dates, this hub stays methodology-first.

If you submit a clear recipe screenshot with visible costs and outputs, we may add a dated “player-confirmed” subsection — still labeled to your version, not as eternal truth.

Crafting narrative & anti-mirror rules

Anime Roblox experiences reuse words like craft, fuse, and evolve. Mirrors copy recipes across place IDs. Our anti-mirror rule: if the station path is not visible in this experience’s UI (place context on About/codes pages), the recipe is Unconfirmed here even if fifty SEO pages agree with each other.

A good crafting session is boring on purpose. Redeem codes, ensure income cards still make sense, open the station, copy the label, spend once, note the output, stop. The excitement of a possible rare is not a source grade. When creators publish a dated recipe post, we can promote Unconfirmed → documented with a stamp. Until then, “everyone knows” is not evidence.

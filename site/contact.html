Contact

Use this page when something on Anime Card Farm Wiki is wrong, outdated, or unclear. We read correction mail; we do not recover Roblox accounts.

Primary inbox

hello@animecardfarm.wiki

That address is the operator contact for editorial corrections, privacy requests, and advertising questions. It is not a place-owner mailbox and not Roblox Customer Support.

What to include (so we can act)

  1. The exact URL (for example /codes or /packs).
  2. The sentence or table row that is wrong.
  3. What you checked: in-game UI label, Roblox experience listing text, or a dated public list with a link and date.
  4. Your timezone if the check time matters (we stamp Asia/Shanghai on money pages).

Response expectations

Money-page errors (wrong Active string, missing Contested) are prioritized over cosmetic typos. We may ask for a clearer screenshot of the UI label, not a cropped “proof” from an unrelated video. If we cannot reproduce the redeem, the row will not silently become Active.

Public surfaces we already watch

  • This site’s codes table and Checked date.
  • Roblox experience listing metadata for the place ID above when we note listing changes.
  • Dated third-party lists only as Contested candidates — never as sole Active proof.

Do not send

  • Account credentials, cookie dumps, or session tokens.
  • Requests to publish a code in exchange for payment or affiliate kickbacks.
  • Mass lists of undated strings with no redeem evidence.

Timezone

Editorial stamps use Asia/Shanghai unless a citation carries its own zone. When you report a redeem time, say which zone you used.

Examples of useful vs useless mail

Useful: “Row on /codes marked Active; redeem failed at 14:10 Asia/Shanghai on place 125039473548047; screenshot shows Expired toast.” That is actionable.

Useless: “codes broken fix asap” with no URL, no string, no time. We cannot replay that.

Useful: “Packs page payback example uses a Shop cost that no longer exists; here is the new label.” We will update method examples.

Useless: “tell me the secret legendary code” — there is no secret channel behind the contact form.

Security hygiene

Never paste .ROBLOSECURITY cookies, password resets, or QR login blobs. If your message includes secrets, we will ask you to rotate them on Roblox and resend a redacted report. We are not a safe place to store credentials.

Press and directories

If you run a directory or another fan wiki and want a reciprocal factual link, email with your URL and what page you are citing. We do not sell Active placements. Directories that strip Contested labels will not get a warm link-back.

Hours and language

Mail is read in Asia/Shanghai business hours when possible; weekend codes emergencies are prioritized around the Saturday-style refresh habit used on sister sites. English is preferred; short clear English beats long untranslated dumps.

After you write us

You may get a short confirmation or a follow-up question asking for a clearer UI crop. Silence for a few days during a large update window does not mean the mail was ignored — money-table disputes still jump the queue.

Escalation for privacy vs codes

If your message mixes a privacy deletion ask with a codes dispute, split them into two emails. Privacy handling may require deleting a thread; codes disputes need the evidence preserved. Subject lines “Privacy” vs “Codes correction” keep those workflows apart.

What success looks like

A good correction ends with a visible page change and an updated Checked date when the table moved. You may not get a long essay reply — the page update is the reply.

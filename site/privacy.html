Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Anime Card Farm Wiki (https://animecardfarm.com) does and does not collect. We are an independent fan guide, not Roblox and not the experience creators.

Who we are

Operator contact for privacy questions: hello@animecardfarm.wiki. If you write about privacy, put “Privacy” in the subject so it is not mixed with code corrections.

What this site is

Static and lightly dynamic HTML guides about a Roblox experience. We publish codes status, beginner routes, pack payback methods, and reference hubs. We do not host the Roblox client and we do not ask for your Roblox password.

Information we may process

  • Server logs — standard hosting/CDN logs (IP address, user agent, URL, timestamps) used for security, abuse detection, and reliability. Retention follows the host’s defaults unless we configure shorter windows.
  • Email you send us — if you contact hello@animecardfarm.wiki, we process the content to reply. Do not include passwords or payment card numbers.
  • Advertising / measurement — when Google AdSense or related tags are active, Google may process device and usage data under Google’s policies. Our ads.txt authorizes pub-7189405305305848. See Advertising disclosure.

What we do not ask for

  • Roblox passwords, session cookies, or 2FA codes.
  • Government ID uploads.
  • Payment card details on this hostname (we are not a shop).

Cookies and similar technologies

Essential delivery may use cookies or similar storage from the CDN. Advertising partners may set cookies when ads are served. Browser controls (block third-party cookies, clear site data) remain available. We do not operate a separate “login account” system on this wiki.

Children

Roblox experiences attract mixed ages. This wiki is written as a general-audience player guide. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for profiles or marketing lists. If a parent believes a child sent personal data to our inbox, email us and we will delete the message thread where feasible.

Your requests

Depending on your region, you may ask what personal data we hold from email contact, or ask us to delete that correspondence. Write to hello@animecardfarm.wiki with enough detail to find the thread. CDN log deletion may be limited by provider retention.

Third-party links

Links to Roblox, Discord, or other guides are outside our control. Their privacy practices apply on those domains.

Changes

We may update this policy when practices change materially. Continued use after a change means you should re-read the new text.

Lawful bases (plain language)

We process contact email because you asked us a question (contract/legitimate interest in replying). We process CDN logs to keep the site secure (legitimate interest in operating a public website). Advertising partners process data under their own policies when ads are shown; you should review Google’s privacy materials if AdSense inventory is active on a page you visit.

International hosting

CDN and DNS providers may process requests in multiple regions. By using a globally distributed static host, your IP may be handled outside your home country as part of ordinary delivery. We do not operate a separate analytics warehouse of player profiles.

Retention sketch

  • Email threads: kept as long as needed to finish the correction or privacy request, then deletable on request where feasible.
  • CDN logs: provider default windows (often days to weeks) unless configured shorter.
  • Published guide text: kept as part of the public archive; personal data should not appear there — if you find your personal info in a page body, mail us for redaction.

Do-not-track and signals

We do not interpret DNT as a separate login preference system because we do not run accounts. Browser tracking protection and consent banners (when the ad stack provides them) are the practical controls.

Security measures

HTTPS via the CDN, security headers where configured, and least-privilege access to deploy credentials. No system is perfect; report suspected site abuse to the contact email with the URL and time.

Automated decisions

We do not run credit scoring or automated ban systems on this wiki. CDN WAF rules may block abusive traffic automatically; that is infrastructure protection, not a player-profile decision inside Roblox.

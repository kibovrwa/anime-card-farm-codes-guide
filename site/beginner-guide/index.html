First-session route

Anime Card Farm Beginner Guide

A full first-session plan for Anime Card Farm: redeem codes, open packs, replace the weakest income slot, upgrade only keepers, then leave so offline income works. Your on-screen income numbers beat any unofficial tier list or YouTube “best card” thumbnail.

Codes → Farm → Offline~first hour
Short answer: Open codes first (Active: NEWPOTIONS!, PRODUCTION!, BRUTALCOMEBACK!, TRAITS!, POTIONS). Then spend Cash to replace the lowest income slot before polishing a card you may swap. Run pack-vs-upgrade payback with your Shop numbers, not a copied spreadsheet.

1. Codes before you spend (2 minutes)

Public October lists still show working strings. We label Active vs Contested on the codes page; we have not redeemed every row in-game.

  1. Open the live table on Anime Card Farm codes.
  2. Redeem Active codes in Shop → Redeem with exact punctuation.
  3. Optionally try each Contested code once.
  4. Skip COMMUNITY / GUILDS expired batches and undated TikTok strings.

Potions and gems from codes are seed capital. Do not dump every Trait potion into the first filler card.

2. Read the farm HUD before buying anything

  • Note Cash per second (or equivalent income rate) on each placed card.
  • Note Shop pack price and upgrade price for your weakest slot.
  • Ignore hype names until those two numbers are visible — early mistakes come from buying a “meta” name that is worse than what you already own.

3. First 10 minutes — three decisions

  1. Open packs until you can compare income on cards you actually own.
  2. Replace the weakest slot when a new card is clearly better. Early Cash exists to change the farm layout, not to stack cosmetics.
  3. Upgrade only keepers after the weak slot is fixed. Then leave the experience so offline money keeps running.

4. Pack vs upgrade (use your numbers)

Payback seconds ≈ cost ÷ income gained per second. If an upgrade returns sooner than the expected pack jump, buy the upgrade. Same logic lives on the packs guide with a worked example you should overwrite with live Shop prices.

5. First-hour checklist

  1. All Active codes redeemed (and Contested tried once).
  2. Weakest slot replaced at least once.
  3. At least one upgrade spent on a card you will still own after the next pack.
  4. No mass-spending of Trait / Rank potions on temporary cards.
  5. Session ends with higher income rate than when you joined — then leave for offline.

6. Common early mistakes

  • Skipping codes and wondering why progression feels slow.
  • Upgrading a card you will replace after the next pack.
  • Trusting undated “best cards” lists over your HUD income.
  • Staying online AFK when offline income would do the same job.
  • Burning expired COMMUNITY / GUILDS codes from old videos.

Honest limits

  • No official public crafting recipe table was confirmed in our source set — see crafting for what we can and cannot claim.
  • Card names on YouTube can lag your live income numbers.
  • Codes expire without notice — recheck /codes after updates.
  • We do not invent drop rates or guaranteed pack EV.

FAQ

What should I do first in Anime Card Farm?

Redeem Active codes, read income on your slots, replace the weakest card, then decide pack vs upgrade with payback math.

Should I buy Robux packs as a beginner?

This wiki does not push paid spend. If you choose to, still run the same payback logic on the Cash/income you care about — paid currency does not remove bad slot choices.

Where is the redeem box?

Shop → Redeem inside the experience. Details on codes.

Minute-by-minute first session (expanded)

  1. 0:00–2:00 — Join place 125039473548047. Open Shop → Redeem. Paste Active codes from /codes. Do not open packs yet.
  2. 2:00–4:00 — Write down income on each placed card and the cheapest upgrade on the weakest slot. Screenshot optional; a note app is enough.
  3. 4:00–8:00 — Open packs only until you have a candidate that beats the weakest slot’s income. Place it. Recalculate.
  4. 8:00–12:00 — Run payback: upgrade keeper vs next pack (formula). Buy the winner once.
  5. 12:00–20:00 — Spend at most one Production/Luck potion if it clearly accelerates the winning path. Leave Trait potions for later.
  6. End of session — Confirm income > start. Leave the experience for offline. Rejoin later instead of idling with the client open if offline pays.

Second session goals

After one good first session, the second join should not restart from zero mentally:

  1. Recheck /codes for Contested flips or new Active strings.
  2. Re-measure income — offline gains may have changed which slot is weakest.
  3. Repeat one payback decision; avoid opening every pack “because you have Cash.”
  4. Only then experiment with mutations or deeper card collection goals.

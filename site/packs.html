Payback calculator logic

Anime Card Farm Packs & Upgrades

Decide whether the next pack or the next upgrade earns its cost back faster. Enter prices and income gains from your Anime Card Farm Shop — not a spreadsheet copied from a video. Codes feed this loop; they do not replace it.

Payback ≈ cost ÷ incomeCodes → Cash
Short answer: Payback seconds ≈ cost ÷ income gained per second. Redeem Active codes first so potions and gems can fund the faster option. If the upgrade on a keeper returns sooner than the expected pack jump, buy the upgrade.

Step-by-step payback

  1. Write down upgrade cost for the card you plan to keep.
  2. Write down income before and after one upgrade (or the UI’s stated gain).
  3. Write down the next pack price.
  4. Estimate pack income gain only from cards you would actually place — ignore collection filler.
  5. Compare payback seconds; buy the smaller number first.
  6. After the purchase, re-measure. Early game numbers change every few packs.

How codes feed packs

  1. Claim Active strings on /codes (NEWPOTIONS!, PRODUCTION!, BRUTALCOMEBACK!, TRAITS!, POTIONS).
  2. Spend Production / Luck potions when they accelerate the winning payback path — not automatically on login.
  3. Trait Gems and ranking potions belong on keepers after the weak slot is fixed (beginner route).
  4. Trade tickets (if a Contested TRADING! still works for you) are optional; they do not replace income math.

When packs lose on purpose

  • Your weakest slot is far behind and a cheap upgrade closes the gap faster.
  • You are about to log out — a small upgrade that raises offline income can beat one more pack open.
  • Pack price spiked after a progression gate and expected income gain is unclear — wait one session rather than guessing EV.

FAQ

Is there an official pack odds table?

We have not confirmed a complete official public odds table in our source set. Until one exists, use income-on-card after the pull — not promised EV from third-party sheets.

Should I always buy the cheapest pack?

Not always. Payback cares about income gained, not sticker price alone.

Do codes change pack strategy?

They change your available Cash/potions timing. The comparison formula stays identical.

Potion timing vs packs

  • Production potions — useful when they raise the income denominator and shorten payback on a keeper you will still own after the next pack.
  • Luck / mutation potions — only after you know what “better” means in income terms; otherwise you are spending fog.
  • Trait / rank potions from codes — post-keeper tools. See beginner resource table.

Anti-patterns

  1. Copying a YouTuber’s pack order without reading your HUD.
  2. Opening packs while a 5-second upgrade would double a weak slot.
  3. Calling a pack “worth it” because of collection completion instead of income.
  4. Treating Contested trade tickets as required progression.
  5. Publishing or trusting fabricated percentage odds on this wiki — we will not host them without an official source.

Checklist before every purchase

  1. Active codes claimed today?
  2. Weakest slot identified with a number?
  3. Upgrade cost and pack cost written down?
  4. Expected income gain for both options estimated from cards you would place?
  5. Winner bought once — then re-measure before the next spend?

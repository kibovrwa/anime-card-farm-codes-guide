Economy: offline, packs, cards

Does Anime Card Farm earn money offline?

Yes. The official Roblox description says packs open and money generates while you are offline. Practical checklist: offline money.

Is there a reliable tier list?

Not from current public official data. Use income shown on your own cards and compare upgrade payback instead of accepting made-up card names or rates. Pack decisions: packs · card literacy: cards.

How do I compare upgrades?

Compare payback time: cost divided by income gained. For a replacement card, the gain is new income minus the income of the card it replaces.

Mutations, traits, CRAFT

When should I use Mutation Potions or Trait Gems?

Wait until the base card is already a keeper. Spending scarce resources on a card the next pack replaces usually wastes them. More: mutations.

What is CRAFT?

The current Roblox title advertises CRAFT, but public platform data does not expose recipes. Read the current in-game menu before consuming cards or materials. Hub: crafting.

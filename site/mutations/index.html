Anime Card Farm Mutations: Potions, Timing & What Not to Invent

Mutations change how cards behave or look after an item or event. This static page teaches an observation loop — not a fabricated multiplier spreadsheet.

Short answer: Use mutation potions from codes on a slot you can measure, change one variable at a time, and write down whether income or UI labels actually moved. If a TikTok lists exact × multipliers with no UI proof, treat it as Reported — not gospel.

Why mutation pages go wrong

  • They paste multiplier tables copied between mirrors.
  • They mix cosmetic mutations with production mutations.
  • They ignore Contested codes that were supposed to grant the potion.

Our rule: describe the test, label uncertainty, and refuse invented × values.

Safe test loop (one change)

  1. Note the card, slot, and any visible income/output indicator before the potion or event.
  2. Apply one mutation source only (one potion, or one weather event — not both).
  3. Wait for the UI to settle; re-read labels on the card and slot.
  4. Compare after. If nothing measurable changed, do not invent a win in your notes.
  5. Only then decide whether a second potion is worth the inventory.

Mutations vs packs

A mutation test is usually cheaper than a pack binge if the potion came from codes. Packs still need payback. Do not burn pack currency to “fix” a mutation rumor — that confounds two experiments.

Crafting & mutations

If crafting UI exists in your client, treat recipes you cannot see as unconfirmed. See crafting for the honesty limits. Third-party craft lists that do not match your labels get ignored.

Potion inventory hygiene

  • Label in your notes which potions came from which Active code check date.
  • Do not dump every potion onto one card in a single minute.
  • Keep one untreated control card on the board when possible so you can see contrast.

When to stop testing

Stop when you cannot remember the before state. Mutation literacy is about memory and notes, not about consuming the whole inventory because a video said “stack everything.”

Documented uncertainty is allowed

Writing “unconfirmed in our client on October 15, 2026” is better than a precise-looking fake grid. Players can live with uncertainty; they cannot live with confident wrong multipliers. If a creator later publishes an official mutation chart, we will cite it with a date and demote rumor tables.

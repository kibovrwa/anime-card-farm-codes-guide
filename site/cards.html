Anime Card Farm Cards: Slots, Weak Links & Replace Order

Cards are the production units on your board. Identify weak slots, replace with purpose, and stop buying packs before the board can use new cards.

Short answer: Redeem codes first, then replace the single weakest producing slot you can measure. Do not clear the whole board after one lucky pull. Pack spend waits until payback math on packs beats a plain production upgrade.

What this page is (and is not)

It is a method page for reading your board. It is not a complete card encyclopedia with invented DPS or rarity multipliers. When the Index or Shop shows a label, trust your client over any frozen web list.

  • We do not publish fabricated per-card income tables.
  • We do not invent fusion trees that are not visible in your UI.
  • We do link the economy loop: codes → board → packs → offline.

First-session card loop

  1. Open the experience place ID 125039473548047 (confirm title matches Anime Card Farm / Factory).
  2. Redeem dated Active codes from /codes before spending early currency.
  3. Look at producing slots — note which card (or empty slot) contributes the least to what you can see.
  4. Replace one weak link. Re-check income or output indicators after that single change.
  5. Only then consider a pack versus a production upgrade using payback.

Reading rarity without a fake tier list

Rarity colors and Index silhouettes help sorting. They are not a purchase order by themselves. A higher rarity card in the wrong slot can still lose to a lower rarity card that actually produces on your board.

  • Sort by whether the card is working, not by how loud the border is.
  • After weather or mutation events, re-read the slot — labels can change meaning.
  • Keep a note of “before” income when you test a legendary replace.

Cards vs packs vs upgrades

Packs add variance. Upgrades add steadier production when the Shop shows clear costs. Cards are the inventory those systems act on. If your board still has empty or idle slots, pack currency is usually premature — see beginner route on beginner guide.

Checklist before you log off

  1. Every slot you care about has a card that is actually producing.
  2. You did not leave Auto-sell (if present) eating cards you meant to test.
  3. Offline readiness checked on offline money.
  4. Mutation experiments documented on mutations if you used potions from codes.

Board audit questions (print or note)

  1. How many slots are empty?
  2. Which single slot looks weakest on visible output?
  3. Did I redeem codes before this audit?
  4. Am I about to buy a pack before filling empties?
  5. What number will I write down before and after the next replace?

Five honest answers beat a 50-row fake tier list.

FAQ

Is there a full card Index on this site? Not as a scraped complete encyclopedia — your client Index is authoritative for silhouettes and names you have unlocked.

Should I sell duplicates immediately? Only after comparing them to your weakest producing slot. Blind auto-sell is how people delete the card that was carrying offline.

Do codes cards bypass payback math? Free cards from Active codes still occupy slots; they do not make every pack positive EV.

Tie-ins across hubs

Cards feed offline readiness and mutation tests. If you only read this page, still open beginner once so the sequence is not pack-first. Pack spend waits on payback. Official surfaces: official links.

When a new card arrives from an Active code, park it in a notes line with the code check date before you auto-sell duplicates. That habit prevents “I had a good card yesterday” amnesia.
